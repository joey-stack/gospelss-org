# Kingdom Partner & Donor Retention Skill

## Purpose
Manages voluntary giving journeys, recurring donor lifecycle ("Kingdom Partner" 150 kr/month), and radical financial transparency benchmarked against Charity: Water.

## Key Capabilities
1. **Recurring Giving Optimization**: Frictionless Stripe subscription enrollment with instant email receipts via Resend.
2. **Domestic Swedish Giving**: High-clarity Swish and Bankgiro payment screens with mobile deep-linking and QR code generation.
3. **Impact Storytelling**: Direct attribution connecting donor support to concert production, venue hire, and artist development grants.
4. **Donor Self-Service**: Stripe billing portal integration allowing donors to update cards, modify amounts, or pause recurring giving effortlessly.
