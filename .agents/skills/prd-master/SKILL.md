# PRD Master Skill

## Purpose
Produces authoritative, comprehensive Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) that bridge high-level vision and developer implementation.

## Standard PRD Structure
1. **Executive Summary & Mission**
2. **User Personas & Journey Maps**
3. **Information Architecture & Sitemap**
4. **Detailed Feature Specifications & Acceptance Criteria**
5. **Data Flow & Entity Relationship Models**
6. **Verification Gates & Launch Criteria**
