# Photography Generator Skill

## Purpose
Generates high-impact, emotionally resonant imagery for GOSPELS event pages, hero banners, and story narratives, specifically tailored to the Swedish gospel and worship context.

## Brand Aesthetics & Visual Style
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Warm Grey ambient light (`#F5F5F3`), deep charcoal shadows (`#1A1918`), and vibrant Tech Turquoise (`#14B8A6`) stage lighting accents.
- **Lighting**: Nordic natural daylight, golden hour open-air settings, and dramatic contemporary worship auditorium haze.
- **Subjects**: Authentic worshipers, expressive gospel choir vocalists, musicians (piano, drums, bass, brass), diverse community attendees in Gothenburg.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Sacred · Welcoming · Transformative. Avoid generic corporate stock visuals; prioritize genuine emotion, hands raised in praise, community warmth, and musical excellence.

## Prompt Templates
- **Open-Air Outreach (*Göteborgs Lovsångskonsert*)**:
  *"Cinematic wide shot of an open-air gospel worship concert in a Gothenburg city square during Scandinavian summer twilight. Diverse crowd gathered with hands lifted in joy, warm tungsten and teal stage lighting illuminating dynamic vocalists on stage. Authentic, heartfelt community atmosphere, shot on 35mm film aesthetic, shallow depth of field, high dynamic range."*
- **Indoor Sacred Gathering (*Holy Spirit Worship*)**:
  *"Intimate, sacred worship gathering in a Scandinavian sanctuary, soft atmospheric haze with turquoise and amber volumetric lighting beams. Powerful acoustic worship, close-up of a gospel vocalist with emotional expression, warm wood architecture, premium contemporary sacred aesthetic."*
