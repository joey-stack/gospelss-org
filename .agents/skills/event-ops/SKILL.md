# Event Operations & Concert Lifecycle Skill

## Purpose
Governs the operational lifecycle for flagship GOSPELS gatherings: *Holy Spirit Worship* (indoor annual gathering) and *Göteborgs Lovsångskonsert* (2027 open-air outreach).

## Operational Slices
1. **Frictionless Registration**: Minimal field RSVP (Name + Email only) with duplicate deduplication.
2. **Automated Reminder Cron**: 24-hour pre-concert countdown email dispatching venue transit details, doors open schedule, and prayer team info.
3. **Volunteer Shift Intake**: Multi-track application routing (Hospitality, Technical Setup, Sound, Media, Pastoral Prayer Team) with mandatory parental consent capture for applicants `< 18`.
4. **Post-Event Archival**: Transitioning completed events into testimony, photo gallery, and media player archives.
