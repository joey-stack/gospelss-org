06 / Interaction

Motion principles

Motion should support meaning, never compete with it.

Reveal · fade up

20px upward movement, opacity 0 → 1, 400ms ease-out. Use on scroll for meaningful content groups.

Hover · gentle lift

200ms ease. Cards lift 4px and gain a soft shadow. Avoid exaggerated zoom or bounce.

Transition · crossfade

300ms fade for page-level changes and media transitions. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Do’s & don’ts

Consistency is how the experience feels intentional.

✓ DO · Warm, clear hierarchy
A place for everyone

Worship brings us together.

✕ DON’T · Harsh or overstyled
Welcome to the event

EVERYTHING IS IMPORTANT!

✓ DO · Use dark surfaces intentionally
Watch & listen

High contrast, calm composition, strong imagery.

✕ DON’T · Use pure black or low contrast

A message gets lost.

Avoid pure black, grey-on-grey text and decorative clutter.
